Company Background & Approach

Structured Formulation Science For Robust Product Development

Successful pharmaceutical development requires a systematic understanding of API physicochemical characteristics, excipient compatibility, process parameters, and critical quality attributes (CQAs).

Quality by Design (QbD)

Systematic screening of critical material attributes (CMAs) and critical process parameters (CPPs) to ensure predictable, reproducible formulation performance.

  • Risk-Based Assessment

Core Services

End-to-End Formulation Development Capabilities

Supporting development journeys from initial API characterisation through to client-side technology handover.

1. Preformulation Studies

Establish the physicochemical and solid-state baseline before initiating compounding.

  • Solubility & pH Profiles
  • Bulk & Tapped Density
  • API-Excipient Compatibility
  • Flow & Compressibility

2. Immediate-Release Tablets

Full-cycle IR tablet development across direct compression, dry granulation, and wet granulation paths.

  • Direct Compression
  • Wet & Dry Granulation
  • Disintegrant Optimisation
  • Dissolution Profiling

3. Hard-Gelatin Capsules

Engineering robust powder, granule, and pellet blend systems for reliable encapsulation.

  • Blend Flow Uniformity
  • Fill-Weight Consistency
  • Shell Compatibility
  • Rupture & Dissolution

4. Modified-Release Research

Exploratory formulation modeling for extended and delayed release mechanisms.

  • Hydrophilic Matrix Systems
  • Polymer Barrier Systems
  • Release Kinetics Profiling
  • Mechanism Modeling

5. Analytical Support

Physicochemical testing and verification coordinating with in-house and qualified external labs.

  • Assay & Content Uniformity
  • Dissolution Testing
  • Related Substances
  • Method Verification

6. Formulation Troubleshooting

Targeted root-cause investigations for formulation or manufacturing defects.

  • Capping & Lamination
  • Sticking & Picking
  • Slow Disintegration
  • Content Inhomogeneity
Laboratory Setup

Planned Infrastructure & Technical Suites

Constructed around safe material movement, containment controls, and contamination mitigation.

Preformulation & Characterisation Lab

  • Physical characterisation, particle assessment, and solubility screening
  • Bulk and tapped density testing, angle of repose, and flow property evaluation
  • Moisture balance, loss on drying, and preliminary compatibility storage

Formulation & Compression Suite

  • Controlled blending, granulating, drying, and sizing instrumentation
  • Instrumented tablet press for compression profiling, weight, and hardness tracking
  • Film coating feasibility setups and in-process testing stations

Capsule Filling & Tooling Suite

  • Precision manual and semi-automated encapsulation equipment
  • Fill-weight variation control, tamping pin, and dosator evaluation
  • De-dusting and polishing evaluation setups

Utilities, Containment & HVAC

  • Zoned ventilation with local dust extraction and containment hoods
  • Continuous temperature and relative humidity monitoring systems
  • Chemical segregation cabinets, solvent safety storage, and compliant effluent controls
